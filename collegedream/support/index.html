CollegeDream Support

简体中文

CollegeDream helps families plan for college together: 118 U.S. colleges with this year's deadlines and cost, a college list with Dream, Target and Safety, and planning tasks, in English and Simplified Chinese.

Contact

Email support@travelpal.now. We usually reply within two business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Can parent and student use it together?

Yes. CollegeDream is made to be shared on one phone.

How do I switch language?

On the first screen, use the language menu at the top. Later, open More on the home screen and choose Language.

A number looks out of date.

Check the college's own page from the link in the app, and email us so we can fix it. Colleges update deadlines and cost each year; we refresh them every summer.

Photos don't appear.

Photos download as you browse, so they need an internet connection the first time.

Is CollegeDream affiliated with any college?

No. Always confirm important dates and requirements with the college.

Read the CollegeDream privacy policy.

CollegeDream 帮助全家一起规划大学申请：118 所美国大学今年的截止日期和费用、可标记冲刺、匹配、保底的选校名单，以及各项规划任务，提供英文和简体中文。

联系我们

邮箱 support@travelpal.now。我们通常在两个工作日内回复。

常见问题

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。所有内容都保存在你的 iPhone 上。

家长和学生可以一起用吗？

可以。CollegeDream 就是为一部手机共同使用而设计的。

怎么切换语言？

在第一个页面顶部的语言菜单中切换。之后可在主页打开“更多”，选择“语言”。

某个数字看起来过时了。

请通过应用中的链接查看大学官网，并发邮件告诉我们以便修正。大学每年都会更新截止日期和费用，我们每年夏天更新一次。

照片不显示。

照片在浏览时下载，第一次查看需要联网。

CollegeDream 与大学有关联吗？

没有。请务必向大学核实重要日期和要求。
