CollegeDream Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · 简体中文

CollegeDream is developed by TravelPal LLC. This policy describes how CollegeDream 1.0 handles your data.

Information You Enter Stays on Your iPhone

Everything you enter in CollegeDream, such as who is using the app, grade, preferences, scores, your college list, notes and task progress, is stored only on your iPhone. CollegeDream has no account. TravelPal does not receive any of it, and does not sell or share it.

College Photos

To keep the app small, college photos download from CollegeDream's own storage as you browse. A request asks only for a photo by its file name and contains nothing about you or your family. Standard network information, such as an IP address, may be processed by the storage provider to deliver the photo; TravelPal does not keep request logs.

Links to Colleges

Links to college websites open in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

No Tracking

CollegeDream does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising identifiers, third-party SDKs, or any form of tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your information stays on your device until you use Start over in the app or uninstall CollegeDream. Either one removes it.

Children's Privacy

CollegeDream is made for high school students and their parents. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, email support@travelpal.now or visit our support page.

CollegeDream 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 29 日

CollegeDream 由 TravelPal LLC 开发。本政策说明 CollegeDream 1.0 如何处理你的数据。

你输入的信息只保存在你的 iPhone 上

你在 CollegeDream 中输入的所有内容，例如使用者、年级、偏好、分数、选校名单、笔记和任务进度，都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。CollegeDream 无需账号。TravelPal 不会收到这些内容，也不会出售或分享。

大学照片

为了让应用保持小巧，大学照片会在你浏览时从 CollegeDream 自己的存储空间下载。请求只按文件名请求照片，不包含任何关于你或你家庭的信息。存储服务商可能会处理 IP 地址等标准网络信息以传送照片；TravelPal 不保留请求日志。

大学网站链接

大学网站链接会在 Safari 中打开。这些网站有各自的隐私政策。

不追踪

CollegeDream 不使用 Cookie、分析工具、广告标识符、第三方 SDK 或任何形式的追踪。

数据保留与删除

你的信息会一直保存在设备上，直到你在应用中使用“重新开始”或删除 CollegeDream。两者都会清除这些信息。

儿童隐私

CollegeDream 为高中生和家长设计。由于它不收集任何个人信息，因此也不会收集儿童的信息。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，变更内容将发布在本页面并更新日期。

联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请发送邮件至 support@travelpal.now，或访问我们的支持页面。
