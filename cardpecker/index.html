Privacy, plainly

  • What's stored: your cards, reward categories, benefits, receipts, and images stay locally on your device.
  • Bank credentials: never requested or stored. CardPecker doesn't log in to your accounts and doesn't need your card numbers.
  • News & Deals: every user receives the same generic feed. The request does not include your wallet, purchases, or other app data.
  • Backups: automatic backups go to your own iCloud Drive and can be turned off; manual backups go wherever you choose in Files.
  • What CardPecker doesn't do: no account, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no data sold or shared.

The full policy is short and readable: CardPecker Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Does CardPecker connect to my bank?

No. CardPecker never asks for bank credentials and never logs in to your accounts. You add your cards and their reward categories manually — no card numbers needed.

How does it know which card earns the most?

You record each card's earning rates by category. When you tap a category, CardPecker compares the effective return across your cards and shows the winner — including subcategory rules like specific airlines or hotel brands.

How do benefits and deals work?

Benefits tracks statement credits and recurring perks. News & Deals is a separate feed of card and travel updates and general savings. Deals are not personalized to your cards. Pair Benefits with our benefit-tracking guide.

Does anything leave my device?

Your card and receipt data stays on your device. News & Deals downloads a generic feed without sending your wallet or purchases. Optional backups use your own iCloud Drive or the Files location you choose. See the privacy policy for details.

How does Worth It work?

Search for a supported program and tap a suggestion. Enter the points or miles needed, the comparable cash price, and any award taxes or fees. Worth It calculates the cash saved per point and compares it with a dated reference valuation. It can search programs that are not linked to your saved cards. See an example.

Is CardPecker financial advice?

No. CardPecker is an organizational tool — it compares the reward rules you enter. It doesn't recommend cards, and nothing on this page is financial, legal, or tax advice.
