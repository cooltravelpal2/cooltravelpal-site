What’s new in DongDong 1.1

Tell DongDong what equipment you have—a chair, a mat, dumbbells or nothing at all—along with your preferred intensity and session length. It suggests a workout that fits and guides you with recorded voices and captions, all offline.

  • Hear what comes next. Your coach announces each upcoming exercise before setup begins.
  • More considered timing. Improved exercise, preparation and recovery timing, with a “+15 seconds” option whenever you need longer.
  • Stay full screen. Playback continues across exercise changes and breaks. Controls float on the right in landscape and at the bottom in portrait, with movie-style subtitles clear of the main demonstration.
  • Watch your way. Choose 3D video or 2D drawings, in full screen or the regular player. Fit mode keeps the whole movement visible with a soft video-frame backdrop instead of dark borders.
  • Keep your choices. Your display preferences stay selected across exercises and future sessions. Choose them during setup or in Settings → Demonstrations.
  • A fresh day’s suggestion. Today refreshes when the day changes and avoids yesterday’s suggestion when another eligible routine exists. Open workouts and saved progress remain intact.

Five coach voices, with English and Mandarin options. No account, ads or tracking. The app’s coaching audio and demonstrations are bundled on your device.

咚咚 1.1：带练更顺畅，观看方式由你选择

选择你现有的器械、训练强度和时长，咚咚会推荐合适的训练，用语音提示和字幕带你完成。五种教练声音，提供英语和普通话选项。语音和示范均可离线使用，无需账号，没有广告，也不追踪用户。

  • 新动作开始前，先用熟悉的教练声音播报名称。
  • 优化动作、准备和休息时长，需要时可增加 15 秒。
  • 全屏播放贯穿动作切换和休息，配上电影式字幕与悬浮控制按钮。
  • 选择 3D 视频或 2D 线条图，铺满屏幕或保留完整画面；完整画面模式以柔和模糊背景取代黑边。
  • 全屏与示范方式会在下次训练时保留，可在初次设置或“设置”中更改。
  • 修复每日训练刷新；有合适替代方案时，避免重复昨天的推荐，同时保留进行中的训练和已保存进度。
