A different home exercise each day

One small preview of DongDong, our home workout app for iPhone and iPad.

Alongside our travel tools, we build DongDong: a home workout app with spoken coaching in English and Mandarin. Its own website now shares one movement each day, with a video demonstration and written instructions.

This page is the daily preview. The complete lesson lives on Dongdong.now, and tomorrow's movement replaces today's.
