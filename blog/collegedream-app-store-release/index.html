CollegeDream Is Here: A Future, One Step at a Time

Meet CollegeDream, now on the App Store: explore 118 U.S. colleges, build your list, and plan together in English and Chinese.

By TravelPalOctober 31, 20262 min read
Introducing CollegeDream

A big dream.
A clear next step.

College planning for students and parents, in English and Chinese.

CollegeDream is now on the App Store. Our new iPhone app helps students and parents explore colleges and take the next step in their college planning, in English or Simplified Chinese.

Start with 118 U.S. colleges. Search in everyday words, explore campus life and admissions information, and save schools to a personal Dream, Target, or Safety list. Compare colleges side by side, then keep moving with eleven planning tasks covering topics such as test preparation, essays, and recommendation letters.

No account is needed and there is no tracking. Your profile, list, and notes stay on your iPhone.

A big decision starts with a small step
