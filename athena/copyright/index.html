The artworks and their pictures

  • The artworks shown are in the public domain, or are shown through a photograph whose licence allows it.
  • Each photograph is credited below to its maker, with its licence and where it comes from. The same credits are on every work's page, under Image credits, and at the end of its film.
  • Pictures are cropped to fit the screen, and a film moves across them. Some close-ups, outlines and diagrams were made for Athena from these pictures.
  • A photograph under a Creative Commons licence stays under that licence. You may reuse the photograph itself as its licence says, from the source linked on the work's page.

The music

The music under the stories comes from freely licensed recordings, each credited below to its performers with its licence.

The museums

  • Facts about a work, such as its date, material, size and room, come from the museum's own published records and from Wikidata.
  • Musée du Louvre. Rooms and what is on display come from the Louvre's collections database, collections.louvre.fr. Opening hours, prices, exhibitions and events are shown as louvre.fr gives them, with the day they were read, and each links to the Louvre's own page. The Louvre's map is offered by link only.
